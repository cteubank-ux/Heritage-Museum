Care and stewardship

Preservation is a shared effort.

Museums have a responsibility to care for materials thoughtfully, provide context, and build respectful relationships with the communities connected to their collections. Support helps sustain this work over time.

For this course prototype, the support options are informational only. There are no payment forms, sign-ups, or personal-data submissions.
